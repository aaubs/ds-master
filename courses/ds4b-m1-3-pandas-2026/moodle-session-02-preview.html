Data Handling and Manipulation with Pandas & NumPy

How do we turn a table into an answer we can trust? We will move from a short Python refresher to NumPy arrays and pandas, then practise cleaning, filtering, grouping, joining and reshaping data. Two historical datasets, an online-course catalog and Spotify playlist data, provide the questions. We will also check what each row represents and what our results can actually tell us.

By the end of the session

  • Translate a loop and an if condition into array operations and boolean masks.
  • Inspect a DataFrame, select rows and columns, and update values with .loc.
  • Make and explain cleaning decisions about missing values, dates and repeated records.
  • Summarize groups with counts, join tables without unexpected row multiplication, and reshape results.
  • Support an answer with a small table or chart and state a limitation of the data.

In class

We will alternate short demonstrations with individual predictions and paired practice. After the Python-to-pandas warm-up, we will work through the Spotify analysis and finish with a brief explanation of one result and one limitation. Two short breaks are included.

The datasets are historical teaching snapshots. The analysis describes those samples, not the current Udemy marketplace or today’s music charts.
