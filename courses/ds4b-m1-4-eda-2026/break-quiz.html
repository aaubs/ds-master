Round 2 The silent album

How much money did an album of pure silence make on Spotify?

In 2014 the band Vulfpeck uploaded Sleepify: ten tracks of nothing at all. They asked fans to play it on loop while they slept. How much did it earn before Spotify took it down? In US dollars. Closest without going over takes the 10 points.

$20,000about · $19,655 paid out

Around 5.5 million plays in seven weeks. Every track was 31 or 32 seconds long, because Spotify only counts a play as a stream after 30 seconds. The band spent the money on a free six-city tour.

Spotify first called it clever, then pulled it for breaking its content rules. Here is what they sound like when they are not silent.

Round 3 The shortest hit

How long is the shortest song ever to chart on the Billboard Hot 100?

The main American singles chart, since 1958. In seconds. Closest without going over takes the 10 points.

34seconds

Jack Black, "Steve's Lava Chicken", from A Minecraft Movie. It entered the Hot 100 at #78 on the chart dated 3 May 2025, and took the record from Kid Cudi's "Beautiful Trip" (37 seconds, 2020), which had taken it from "PPAP" (45 seconds, 2016).
