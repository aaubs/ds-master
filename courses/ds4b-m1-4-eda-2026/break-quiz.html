Nobody guesses A. Hotline Bling has 1.7 billion streams and this file scores it zero, alongside Taylor Swift's "22" and four Eminem records.

So a zero in that column does not mean nobody listened. It means Spotify had no figure for that track when the snapshot was cut, and the column stores absence in exactly the same shape as a real measurement. That is 2,620 tracks, nine percent of the catalogue, sitting inside every average we have quoted today.

The most energetic track in a dataset of 28,356 songs is a recording of rain falling on leaves. The least energetic is crickets near a waterfall, and the most instrumental is waves and wind. All three sit on a latin playlist, inside any average anybody quotes.

Energy is a signal-processing measure of loudness, density and noisiness. It was never a claim about excitement, and a downpour scores maximum on all three. Write "edm is the most energetic genre" and you have said something about how the recordings were mastered.

Three rounds, three different ways of being wrong about the same file.

Round 1: the data does not cover the world you think it covers. Round 2: a column can store "we don't know" and "zero" in the same slot and never tell you which is which. Round 3: a measurement is whatever its definition says, not whatever its name suggests.

None of those show up in a p value. All three change what you are allowed to write down, which is the whole argument of this session.
